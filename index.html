AVantage

AVantage is a personal relationship assistant. Its owner uses it to keep track of professional relationships (when they last spoke with someone, follow-ups they have set, and public news about the people and firms they choose to follow) so they have timely reasons to reconnect.

AVantage is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else, there is nothing to sign up for, and the only Google account it connects to is its owner's.

Privacy policy

AVantage privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

This policy explains what data AVantage accesses, including data from Google APIs, how that data is used, stored and protected, and how it can be deleted. In this policy, "the owner" means the single person who runs AVantage and whose Google account it connects to.

Google permissions requested

AVantage requests only the following permissions, and only for the owner's own Google account:

  • See your primary Google Account email address (userinfo.email): to confirm that the account signing in is the owner's. Any other account is rejected.
  • See your contacts (contacts.readonly): to read the owner's Google Contacts (names, email addresses, phone numbers, organizations and job titles) so they can be matched to the owner's relationship records.
  • See, edit, download and permanently delete your contacts (contacts): to add a new contact or update a contact's name, email, phone, employer or title. AVantage makes a change only after the owner reviews and approves that exact change. It never deletes or merges contacts.
  • Read your email messages and settings (gmail.readonly): to read messages to and from a list of specific email addresses the owner has approved, so the app can record when the owner last corresponded with those people. AVantage cannot send, delete, label or change email.

What data is collected and how it is used

  • Contacts: contact details are stored so the owner can see each person's details alongside their own notes and history, and so proposed corrections can be compared with what is in Google.
  • Gmail: for messages exchanged with approved addresses only, AVantage stores the date, sender, recipients, subject and message text. It does not download file attachments. Messages that do not involve an approved address are not read.
  • News searches: for people and firms the owner has chosen to follow, AVantage sends the person's name and firm name, or the firm name alone, as a search to Google News. No email content, email addresses or other contact details are included. Only the headline, link, source and date of each result are stored.
  • The data is used only to show the owner their own relationship records, reminders and relevant news, and to carry out contact changes the owner has approved. It is not used for advertising, for profiling, or for any purpose unrelated to these features.

Storage and security

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's computer, in a private data folder kept separate from the app's code. AVantage has no server or cloud database of its own.
  • The app's interface is available only on the owner's computer, not over the network, and each session uses a private, randomly generated link.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept in the same private folder and are used only to make the requests described above.
  • Backups are stored on the same computer. If the owner turns on off-device copies, each copy is encrypted with a passphrase only the owner holds before it is saved to the owner's own cloud storage.

Sharing

Data from Google is not sold, rented, shared with or transferred to any third party, and nobody other than the owner can see it. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. Apart from Google's own APIs, the only outside service contacted is the Google News search described above.

AI and machine learning

Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Older copies of the contact list are removed automatically once newer copies are saved, and news results that have not been reviewed are removed after 30 to 90 days.
  • The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the AVantage data folder and its backups.
  • Access to Google can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, AVantage can no longer read or change anything in the Google account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AVantage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If AVantage starts using data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that happens, and the effective date above will change.

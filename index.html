AVantage

A personal tool its owner uses to keep track of professional relationships. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Privacy policy

AVantage runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner.

What it accesses

  • Google Contacts: reads the owner's contacts, and changes a contact only when the owner reviews and confirms that exact change.
  • Gmail: read only, and limited to messages to and from email addresses the owner has approved.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's computer, with an optional encrypted backup the owner controls. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Google user data

AVantage's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
